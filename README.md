# tcc-advent

The LOST door for the TCC advent calendar (Kay & Dax, 2026).

- `index.html` — the door page (tap the slate, get the reel)
- `LOST.pdf` — the brief a member hands to their AI
- `lost_kit.py` — helper that makes the title cards and stitches the film
- `cast/` — numbered chibi references; the key is in the PDF
- `source/` — draft 4 of the brief and the script that turns it into `LOST.pdf`
